### How to measure

1. Place a piece of paper on the floor against a wall.
2. Step on the paper with your heel lightly touching the wall behind you.
3. Mark the end of your longest toe on the paper.
4. Measure from the wall to the mark.

*If you fall between two sizes, we recommend sizing up for a more comfortable fit.*
